# Internal review

Root chooses a stable review boundary and sends a fresh read-only Sol reviewer the bounded paths, exclusions, governing requirements, stable input marker and validation expectations. Do not pass author reasoning, prior findings or scores into an independent full review. The target must not be modified during review.

Review covers material defects with anchored evidence, concrete impact and verification. Exclude taste and speculative refactoring. Root tracks open findings and assigns bounded repairs; reviewer does not supervise repair or create Scout. Repeat a full review only when repair materially changes the reviewed behavior, contract, architecture or risk boundary.
